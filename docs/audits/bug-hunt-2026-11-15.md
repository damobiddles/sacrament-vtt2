# Bug hunt and polish, 15 November 2026

The owner asked for a full bug hunt after the past week's changes, with more feedback on rolls, less double handling and a smoother combat flow. The prompt used is in the project folder (`audits/2026-11-15/bug-hunt-and-polish-prompt.md`) and is now the weekly routine.
Tested locally in desktop Chrome at 1366×800, as the GM and as a player on a fresh table, and in the solo game. Not verified on phones, Safari or Firefox, and not checked on the live site. No rules, numbers or stored key names were changed.

## Bugs fixed (each with a test that failed first)
- **A hit said "SUCCESS", but a miss said "MISS"** (table and solo). A player's hit with a weapon now says **HIT**. Three older tests had written down the old word; they now expect HIT.
- **The latest-activity line cut off its second line and its Undo button.** Two lines and the Undo button now fit.
- **The map jumped when the damage step appeared.** The label "Own die for damage" sat above its box and made the bottom strip taller. It now sits beside the box, as the d20 one does.
- **The rising total covered the verdict** on the result card ("AGAINST TARGET 12" over "FAILED CHECK"), at the table and in solo.

## Roll feedback (table and solo, one shared card)
- The card keeps the **whole sum** once the floating numbers have gone: "15 + 3 STR = 18 against Target 12", with Easy or Hard when that changed the Target.
- It says **by how much**: "Made it by 6", "Missed by 3", or "Made it exactly". This is left out when a natural 20 or a misfire decided the roll.
- The **verdict waits** until the total has popped over the die, then pops in. The numbers rise first, then the card says what they mean.
- A **natural 20** makes the card glow once, and a **natural 1** makes it shudder once. Nothing moves when the device asks for reduced motion. These are CSS only, with no new files or libraries.

## Combat flow and consistency
- With a foe selected, a player's **ways to deal with it come straight under it**. Before, their own HP and sheet button came first and pushed the actions below the fold. The trade-off: while a foe is selected, your own HP − and + buttons are further down (scroll, or press Back). The walk-through test now scrolls to a button before pressing it, as a person would.
- A hero's **weapons and items are solid buttons**. They were drawn dashed and dim, like something switched off. One that can't be used now keeps the dashed look.
- The **damage button** in the bottom strip has the same padding as the buttons beside it (its text touched the edges).
- **Easy / Normal / Hard** are capitalised like every other button.
