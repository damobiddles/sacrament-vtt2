# Table (VTT) audit, 11 November 2026

Asked by the owner: "check all aspects of the VTT including rules, where buttons appear in the UI and is navigation optimised? Could we add more player feedback? Fix anything that needs fixing."
Done as a GM and as a player in desktop Chrome (1366×800) on a local table. Not verified on phones, Safari or Firefox.

## The prompt used

1. **Rules.** Every number, roll and wording on the table must match the shared rules (`rules.js`, `sheet-core.js`, the rules pages), including PRs #72 (the Horror at the heart), #73 (ways through an obstacle), #75 (no tags shown to players) and #76 (rising numbers).
2. **Buttons.** The button you need most is where your eye and mouse already are; labels are plain; nothing needed is hidden behind a scroll; dangerous buttons are apart from common ones.
3. **Navigation.** Clicks for the common jobs (join, roll, attack, take damage, end turn, change scene, give loot); dead ends; losing your place.
4. **Feedback.** After every action, can you tell it worked? Confirmations, turn cues, hit and miss, Horror rising, saving and offline. Build on #76; sound only as an opt-in, off by default.
5. **Fix** clear bugs and usability problems in one PR, with tests. Ask first before any rule change or big redesign.

## Rules: what was checked

Matches the shared rules: Horror (push 1 after any failed roll, natural 1 only on magic or a pushed action, 1 HP a point of blood past 21, thresholds 7/14/21 once an adventure, New adventure), Party rest d6, dying 3 rounds with the free CON crawl and 1 Horror to act, path Easy once a scene (not on attacks), two-handed +2 damage, guns misfire on 1 to 3, no tags or Hinder/Stop shown to players.

Mismatches found and fixed (display only):
- **Standalone Effort offered "Ultimate d12"** as a kind of Effort. Since #72 Ultimate is only the critical die. It now offers Basic, Weapons, Guns and Magic.
- **The sheet said "+2 heavy weapon"**; the rule and the table say two hands. It now says "+2 two hands".
- **Ways through an obstacle (#73)** were not said at the table. After a successful check on a card with an Effort bar, the panel now says which way sets which die.

## Fixed: feedback

- **"Your turn, *name*." and "*X* hits you" banners had stopped appearing** (only the chime and the tab title were left, though the README and tests describe them). They show again, after the dice land, for six seconds.
- **The Horror passing 7, 14 or 21** was only one line in the feed for players. It is now a notice on the table ("…your next roll is Hard.").
- **Shaken was invisible:** the server never sent it, so nobody knew a hero's next roll would be Hard until it happened. It is now sent to everyone and shown beside Defence and beside the roll buttons; it goes when the roll uses it.
- **At 0 HP**, the roll buttons now say the CON crawl is free and any other roll costs 1 Horror, before the press.
- **The Horror meter** pulses and shows the change (+1, −4) on every screen when it moves.
- **Opt-in table sounds:** a small click as each die lands (a bright one for a natural 20, a low one for a 1) and a low tone when the Horror rises. Off unless switched on; the switch is beside the dice switch (players: Dice and sound; GM: Table tab).

## Fixed: buttons and navigation

- **After a push, the old MISS card stayed over the map** until Continue was pressed. It goes once the push is taken.
- **The GM's Horror meter** in the top bar now opens the Horror controls (Table tab).
- **Players were told to "choose … in Play"**, but players have no Play tab. The words now point to "Choose an action", the right-hand panel or the bottom strip.
- **The map jumped** whenever the latest-activity line grew from one line to two. That line now keeps one height (two lines; more scroll).
- **"Roll digitally"** was cut off in the player's own-d20 box; the box is wider.

## Ideas the owner approved and that were then built (same PR, 11 November)

1. **"I'm done" for players**: a button that lights the GM's Next turn. Only the GM advances turns (owner, 24 Oct); this would only signal.
2. **Paths on the sample heroes** (for example Thorne Mercenary, Wisp Scholar, Brother Anselm Priest, Tilda Hunter), so the "Fits my path" button shows on a new table.
3. **Next turn from any GM tab** (today it is only in Play, by the owner's decision of 25 Oct).
4. **A "Good idea" button on obstacle cards** that fills half of what is left, once (#73 leaves it to the GM by hand).
5. **Push on the MISS card itself**, as well as in the panel (the reveal card was kept to one button on purpose).
6. **Keyboard shortcuts** for players (1 to 6 for the stats, Enter to roll).

The owner approved all six the same evening; see the entry in `docs/vtt/owner-decisions.md`.
