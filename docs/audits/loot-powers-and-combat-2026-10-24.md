# Loot powers and combat follow-up — 24 October 2026

This follows the rules/range audit. The owner authorised completing its recommended work in order: define unfinished loot powers, control Push exceptions, implement area/scene powers, balance crossbows and critical damage, then run a focused playtest. The earlier audit is a record of the previous release; this document supersedes its open findings in these areas.

## Result

1. **Defined powers.** The Thorned Reliquary, Last Rites, binding-seal shard, bone flute, holy water, snare, physician's satchel, fingerbone, warden bell, ashen rosary, drowned lantern, stained glass and hound-tooth charm now give an explicit action, check where relevant, range, target, result, duration/limit, Horror cost and GM boundary. The flute distracts rather than dealing damage; a snare is a physical trap resolved by the GM. The physician's satchel describes the d8 healing the engine already performed. Information, eligibility, conditions, placement and escape remain GM rulings.
2. **Push.** Player Push requires the hero's active turn and latest failed attack in the current exchange. The GM may explicitly grant an exception from the selected hero's panel. It still costs one Horror, cannot be granted twice, and the resulting extra action cannot itself Push. Without turn order, a GM exception is required. Changing scene clears a pending second action; old failed attacks cannot be banked across exchanges. Ordinary checks cannot Push.
3. **Area and scene abilities.** Rend the Veil selects its visible foes before casting. One check, one damage roll and one Apply affect all selected foes together. The server validates every target before any HP change. One Undo restores the damage to all victims and corrects the recap. Hidden targets are refused and removed from player projections if later hidden. The caster's once-per-scene allowance is spent on an attempt, even a miss, and is shared across copies. The binding-seal shard is also tracked, and the Executioner's Blade has a separate final-blow action (3 Horror, Ultimate, once per scene). Rest, reconnect and switching away/back do not refresh allowances. The GM's confirmed Refresh scene abilities begins a new encounter and invalidates unfinished limited rolls; completed damage is retained. Undoing an attempt restores its allowance only after its damage is undone. Horror has its own existing GM Undo entry; restoring an allowance does not silently refund Horror.
4. **Balance defaults.** Crossbows add +2 accuracy, use Weapons d6, require two hands and still cost a Move to reload. Bows do not receive the bonus and keep their Move. The designer has an explicit crossbow style; old authored custom ranged weapons retain their existing style. A successful natural 20 now adds an extra Ultimate die to the normal damage/Effort roll when the table option is on. Normal and Ultimate bonuses count once each; an Ultimate base counts its bonus once in total; heavy +2 counts once. House dice remain supported. Earlier pending rolls retain their old replacement rule and completed history is not rewritten. Monster natural-roll hit/miss rules and the existing critical-healing engine are unchanged.
5. **Focused playtest.** Disposable GM/player browser sessions exercise the new flows, shared damage, reconnect, Undo, scene refresh and off-turn Push. This verifies software behaviour, not enjoyment or long-term balance. A real group should try the new powers and crossbow trade-off next.

The owner was offered choices for crossbow balance and critical damage. With no response while independent work continued, the recommended defaults above were explicitly stated and implemented under the authority to make routine decisions. No item IDs, counts, slots, book spell assignments or loot probabilities changed. The catalogue remains 178 items / 23 spells / 16 grimoires. Public rules, player/GM guides and the game summary were updated with the implementation. `/loot/` continues to build from the same shared catalogue; no parallel hand-maintained loot list was added.

## Validation and remaining limits

New regressions cover permissions, hidden targets, atomic application, retry deduplication, one damage application, shared Undo, scene allowances across rest/reload/scenes, invalidated old encounters, copied powers, old pending critical rolls, critical bonuses, crossbow accuracy and catalogue invariants. Old ordinary-damage walkthroughs now use a non-critical 15; their damage assertions remain intact. Tests for the superseded replacement-critical rule and catalogue snapshots were updated to the reviewed rules, with a separate fingerprint preserving IDs, slots, spell assignments and odds. No test or accessibility threshold was removed or loosened and no timeout was raised.

Local evidence: `/workspace/sacrament-audit/powers-*.log` and `powers-area-picker.png` / `powers-area-damage.png`. Release checks passed: lint/type checking, 456 unit tests, quick contrast, 142 laptop browser tests (one existing skip), both full laptop contrast sets, 65 table scenarios, 75 reliability walkthrough checks, 26 hero/conversion checks and the required phone smoke test. The one outdated prototype assertion requiring replacement-critical text was corrected and that affected suite rerun; the other passing suites were not repeated. Live publication is verified separately after pushing main.

The GM still judges range, eligibility, line of sight, esoteric outcomes, conditions, ammunition, reloads and Move/Action budgets. Existing custom free-text powers are not silently converted. Full phone suite, physical devices, Safari, assistive technology and a human group playtest are outside this routine release check.
