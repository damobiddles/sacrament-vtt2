## Bugs fixed (each with a test that failed first)
- **Solo: a dying hero who acted anyway and hit lost the hit.**
  - Acting while dying costs 1 Horror. The attack hit and the dice card offered Roll damage, but pressing it did nothing. The button went grey, and the only choice left was Crawl away.
  - At the table, the damage of an action already paid for costs nothing more. Solo now does the same: Roll damage works, then the foe moves.
  - This is what stuck one of this week's solo play-throughs.
- **Solo: a dying hero who acted anyway and missed was told "You may push for a second action, or end your turn"**, but was offered neither, only Crawl away.
  - Now the push choices and End your turn are both there.
  - A dying hero's push costs the push and the Horror for acting while dying, and the label says so ("Push: … (dying: +1 Horror)"). That is 2 Horror in all, the same as at the table, which charges 1 for the push and 1 for the second roll.
  - Free uses (the Penitent's Chain) are not offered while dying, as before.
- **A new parity test** plays the same moments at the table and in solo, and checks that the Horror is the same: acting while dying, the damage of a hit, and a push after a miss.
