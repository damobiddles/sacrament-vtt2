# Bug hunt and consistency audit, 16 November 2026

This is the weekly routine. The prompt is in the project folder at `audits/2026-11-15/bug-hunt-and-polish-prompt.md`. Screenshots are in `audits/2026-11-16/`: `before/` has the play-through, solo and site walk, and `after/` has the fixes.

Testing was local, in desktop Chrome at 1366×800:
- **The table:** a fresh table with the GM and two players. It covered a hit and its damage, a miss, a natural 20, a natural 1, a fall to 0 HP and a death, the Horror past 7, 14 and 21, a new adventure, a rest, Undo, a reload, and going offline and back.
- **Solo:** played to the end twice, dying once and winning once. 400 more random runs were played by script.
- **The website:** all 35 pages.

Not verified on phones, Safari or Firefox, or on the live site. No rules, numbers or stored key names were changed.

The full test suite passed before the fixes and again after them.

## Bugs fixed (each with a test that failed first)
- **A dead hero's player was still offered every action.** This covered weapons, checks, the stat buttons, and the Effort still waiting from a roll made just before the fall. The table refuses all of them ("Thorne is dead."), so every press ended in an error. The player now sees "Thorne has died. The dead cannot act.", the box for their last words, and I'm done when it is their turn. If the GM gives a round back, the choices come back.
- **Solo: a foe's blow on a dying hero rolled a damage die that did nothing.** The log said "(d6 shows 3)" and the dice card said "3 damage", but the blow only cost a round. The table rolls no damage there. Solo now does the same: only the d20 is rolled, and the card says "No damage at 0 HP: the blow costs a round."
- **The owner's overview (`/inventory/`) listed `/app.js`**, which was removed earlier. It now lists the shared files that exist, including `/analytics.js`. A test checks that every listed file is really there.

## Roll feedback
- **The result card now says why a roll was Easy or Hard**, at the table and in solo: "against Target 9. Easy (fits the path)" or "Hard (shaken)". In solo it can also say "Hard (in the dark)". The activity line already said this in full, but the card only said "Easy" or "Hard". When the GM picks Easy or Hard, the card gives no reason. Two older solo tests had written down the plain "Hard"; they now expect "Hard (shaken)", and one checks for any Hard.
- **Already in place and checked this week:**
  - The die, each modifier, the sum, the Target, "Made it by" or "Missed by", and the verdict.
  - A natural 20 makes the card glow and a natural 1 makes it shudder.
  - The button for what to do next (Roll damage, Continue).
  - The Horror a spell costs ("The Horror rises 1 (now 1 of 21)") is in the activity line, not on the card.

## Click counts (desktop, player)
A player's attack with a weapon takes five presses: the foe, the weapon, Roll, Roll damage, I'm done. A miss on your own turn offers Push. Off your turn it doesn't, which is the rule (the GM can allow it). Nothing was found that adds presses since last week.
