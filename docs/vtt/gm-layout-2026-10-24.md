# GM layout — 24 October 2026

The owner resumed the previously held GM redesign. This is a screen/workflow change; server permissions, game rules, saved tables and character storage remain unchanged.

- The main GM view contains GM tools and standalone GM dice, without a player action strip, an Acting as selector, or an automatically marked acting hero.
- The header explicitly labels GM view or Player view. Oxblood and verdigris accents distinguish the two, with readable text labels so colour is not the only signal.
- Enemies and obstacles retain Attack a hero and their damage workflow. GM dice support shared and GM-only results without needing a hero.
- To help a player, select their hero and open their sheet. Ready to use retains equipment/spell actions; Roll for offers the six stat checks with difficulty and optional physical d20. These are ordinary checks; item attacks keep their existing target/damage flow.
- At narrow widths the map and GM tray have separate, non-overlapping boxes inside one scrolling area. At laptop widths the tray remains beside the map. The dice footer has its own height limit and scrolling if needed.
- Player controls and server authority remain intact. Only the GM advances turns.

The release adds desktop workflow coverage at 683×500, 1000×500 and 1366×768, plus role, dice privacy and session-switching checks. Existing dialog tests use the selected hero's sheet and retain their focus-trap, focus-return, reflow and accessibility assertions. The compatibility walk-through checks the new GM view rather than expecting the removed player strip.
