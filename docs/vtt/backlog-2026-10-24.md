# Backlog implementation pass — 24 October 2026

The owner authorised the listed backlog in order. This records the implementation and its limits; deployment is verified separately.

1. **Phone/Safari:** the full simulated-phone suite was requested. Fixed excess top-bar spacing and restored compact footer/feed spacing so the map and tray retain room. Updated two obsolete Items-button tests to exercise the current sheet quick actions and d6 random-book selection. WebKit is unavailable in this environment; its download was denied (HTTP 403). No further download attempts are planned. Chromium emulation does not verify Safari or physical devices.
2. **Healing:** known catalogue bandages, Mend, Aqua Vitae, Saint's Knucklebone and the Hospice Saint's Rib resolve on the server. Select self or another visible HP target within the narrative range; the table does not measure range. Drinking heals the drinker. Healing is capped at maximum HP, stands up downed heroes and never raises a mechanically dead hero. A fallen caster cannot act. Bandages Tend a dying hero on an Easy check, with d4 healing on a natural 20; ordinary successful healing uses the stated die, adding d12 on a natural 20 only when Ultimate Effort is enabled. Recipient/source allowances survive refresh, cannot be bypassed by changing caster, and reset on Party rest. Downed recipients retain the written exception. Failed checks spend item charges but no healing allowance. Refused uses spend neither. Duplicate actions apply once. Undo restores HP, charges and allowance; undo later related healing/rests first. Healing spells still have no casting Horror; relics keep their costs and a natural 1 remains a separate general Horror point.
3. **Scene danger:** an opt-in Play switch adds max(0, Target − 11), matching the existing draft arithmetic (+1 at 12, +3 at 14, +5 at 16). Off by default, including on old saves, to preserve existing manually adjusted monster bonuses. The GM must remove any manually included scene component before enabling it. No saved bonus is rewritten.
4. **Character shortcut:** player footer says My character and appears only for an owned hero. The GM shortcut says Character sheet when acting as a hero.
5. **Reliability:** existing multi-window recovery/retry checks plus a new six-hour logical-clock test across service recreation verify credentials, duplicate handling, persistence and privacy. This is simulated time, not six hours of live observation. The historical seven-second delay remains unreproduced; hours on a warm Netlify instance remain unverified.
6. **Earlier audit:** simultaneous score/Effort stepper edits now send increments, checked against current server state and the build budget. Concurrent uploads recheck both count and total bytes inside the conditional save and delete only their own rejected upload blob. Import identity/privacy checks passed without changing the import rules. Custom dice labels now use the table's actual settings in the sheet and loot designer. Owner explicitly chose monster natural 1 = always miss and natural 20 = always hit; implemented and tested without adding monster critical damage.
7. **Accessibility:** a shared, type-checked dialog controller makes the background inert, confines Tab/Shift+Tab, preserves the separate recovery warning and restores keyboard focus after close. Automated WCAG checks and narrow desktop reflow checks cover the sheet. Real screen-reader and Safari/physical-device checks remain outstanding.
8. **Practical improvements:** first-session guidance and feedback links, a Resources download hub for the existing approved original artwork and a corrected direct VTT link, an update note on the earlier roadmap post, and a GitHub Actions workflow for the existing full release checks. The workflow does not itself configure branch protection or gate Netlify publishing. No new artwork or broader reuse licence is implied. Damage totals and hardest blow in session recaps now remove undone damage; other recap lists and the Horror peak remain historical event records. Historical recap data is preserved. Shared dialog extraction and broader type checking improve maintenance without a wholesale rewrite.

Known healing items retain a validated healingSource when newly copied through the GM loot designer. Existing free-text custom copies are kept intact and remain manual; their rules cannot safely be inferred from their names. Other free-text custom effects, range and narrative rulings remain with the GM.

Validation: reproducing regression tests preceded fixes for focus escape, healing, scene danger, concurrent increments, upload limits, monster natural rolls, recap Undo, hidden healing information and healing house-rule/difficulty handling. Existing tests were not weakened, and existing time limits were not increased. Only disposable tables are used for live verification.
