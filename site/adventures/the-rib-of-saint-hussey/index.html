The Rib of Saint Hussey

One small bone, one day's road, a canon who wants it back, and a village that has waited a hundred years for its feast.

The job

A hundred years ago, when the sickness came to Aubrecht, a lay brother called Hussey walked in from the village of Wendholt with a handcart and carried the dead out of the city for forty days. Then he caught it, walked home and died. The city made him a saint, dug him up and kept his bones in the cathedral of Saint Aubrel's, where the sick pay good coin to be let near them.

Wendholt has finally won him back. The Cardinal has ordered his rib returned to his chapel and laid in his tomb at dusk tomorrow, on his feast day. Wendholt is a hard day's road east, over the River Wend. Wendholt sent Sister Eulalia to fetch him, and Sister Eulalia has hired the heroes: a purse of clipped coin each, a seat at the feast, and the parish's eternal thanks.

“Four hundred people are roasting a pig for a man who has been dead a hundred years. I am not going home without him.”Sister Eulalia of Wendholt

Canon Rainald Pell, the sacrist of Saint Aubrel's, keeps the treasury and the coin the rib brings in. He has had a copy made (a matching case, a pig's rib on linen) and means to swap it in before the real one reaches Wendholt. He tries in the crowd (scene 1), at the gate (scene 2) and on the road (scene 3). If all of that fails, he takes his coach by the bridge road and gets to Wendholt first (scene 5).

The rib

It is the catalogue relic Rib of the Hospice Saint, unchanged: yourself or a target in Close or Near heals d8, once per person per rest, for 2 Horror (1 if the bearer walks the Barber-surgeon's path). Give it to whichever hero carries it. It takes one slot, counts towards their three Horror-tempting items and only works while equipped. It is a fine thing to have in a running fight. It has one side effect the heroes find out at the river.

The people

Sister Eulalia

Nineteen, quick, sharp-tongued, rides a mule called Patience that has none. Knows every saint and every short cut. Fights with a stick and her opinions.

Canon Rainald Pell

Soft hands, gold thread, a voice like a good bell. Never fights. Always has papers. Sincerely believes the rib does more good in a cathedral.

Wat Gilder

A thief with a satchel and very fast feet. Paid by Pell's steward. Talks the moment he is caught.

Konrad Hask

Pell's hired rider. Former road warden, polite, tired, deadly with a crossbow. Makes an offer before he shoots.

The Snatch

Target 12Aubrecht: the cathedral steps, the fish market, the roofs

Every bell in Aubrecht is ringing. The cathedral steps are packed with pilgrims, cripples, pickpockets and pie-sellers. Canon Pell reads the Cardinal's order in a voice like honey and puts a small wooden case into your hands. The crowd surges forward to touch it. Something tugs at your arm. A boy has cut the strap, and a thin man is already three steps away with the case under his coat and running.

Start in the action: the case is gone, Wat Gilder is running, and the chase is on. Down the steps, through the fish market, up a cart onto the roofs of the tanners' row.

The Gate

Target 12The east gate of Aubrecht

The east gate is jammed with carts. The watch are stopping every one of them and climbing in. A sergeant at the arch is reading a paper with a cathedral seal on it, and the description he reads out to his men sounds a great deal like you.

Pell has told the watch that thieves have stolen a relic of Saint Aubrel's and are trying to leave by the east gate. It is not quite a lie: the heroes now carry the cathedral's copy too. Let the players choose how to get out. Every plan is fine. Each one is a different way of filling the same obstacle.

The Riders

Target 13The east road, a long hill down to the river

A carter has given you a lift on a hay wagon in exchange for a blessing from the saint. You are near the top of a long hill when you hear hooves behind you. Three riders. The one in front wears an old road warden's coat, and he has a crossbow across his saddle. He comes alongside, touches his hat, and smiles.

Konrad Hask makes his offer at a canter: hand over the case, take a purse that would buy a house, and the village still gets a rib in a box. Who would know? If the heroes refuse, he shoots the carter's horse in the harness, and the wagon goes down the hill with nobody steering it.

Rest: the heroes can take a Party rest in the hedge, if they want to arrive at the river after dark.

Brackford Ferry

Target 13The River Wend, at dusk

The Wend is high and brown. The ferry is a flat barge on a rope, and the ferrywoman hauls you out into the current, singing. Halfway across she stops singing. Hands are coming up over the side of the barge. White hands, wet hands, dozens of them, reaching, and every one of them is reaching for the case.

For a hundred years the poor who could not pay the cathedral walked to Wendholt to pray at Hussey's empty tomb instead. Many tried to ford the Wend. Now the saint is crossing the river at last, and the drowned have come up to meet him. The common folk call them Gaunt Penitents. They are not angry. They want to touch him, and they will pull anyone into the river to do it.

The Penitents' rules

GM rulings for this adventure, not table rules.

  • Steel drops them, and they slide back into the river. Holy water, the Candle of Last Rites and Holy Oil work on them as their own words say.
  • The rib lays them to rest. Use its power on a Penitent and the healing ends it: it touches the case, says a name, and goes still. That costs the rib's usual Horror (2, or 1 for a Barber-surgeon). The heroes will work this out fast, and it will tempt them.

If the heroes lay at least one to rest, the rest stop fighting and simply wade after the barge, at a distance, heads bowed. They follow the rib to Wendholt. That is the proof in scene 5.

The Feast at Wendholt

Target 13The village green and Saint Hussey's chapel, the next evening

You hear Wendholt before you see it: fiddles, drums, a whole village singing badly. There are bonfires on the green and a pig on a spit. Then the crowd sees Sister Eulalia and goes quiet. Up by the chapel door, beside a cushion with a small wooden case on it, a man in white and gold turns and spreads his arms in welcome. Canon Pell got here first.

Pell came by the bridge road in a closed coach with two cathedral sergeants. He has told Wendholt that the heroes are relic thieves, that poor Sister Eulalia was fooled, and that he has brought the true saint himself out of love for the village. His case has the sacristy seal. Father Rook, the old priest, wants to believe him. Reeve Gisela Moor does not trust anyone from the city, Pell included.

Laying him down

When the village is won, Sister Eulalia carries the rib into the chapel and lays it in the tomb that has stood open for a hundred years. If the Penitents came, they lie down on the green, one by one, and are only bodies. Then the fiddles start again, slower. Wendholt will bury its pilgrims in the morning. Tonight it eats the pig.

If the heroes took Hask's deal

Pell's copy goes into the tomb, and the feast is lovely. Nothing comes up from the river. Hask rides back to Aubrecht with the real rib, and the drowned walk after it all the way. A week later, the cathedral of Saint Aubrel's starts closing its doors at dusk, and nobody will say why. That is the next adventure.

Rewards

  • A purse of clipped coin each, as promised, and a seat at every feast of Saint Hussey for life.
  • The Saint's Knucklebone (relic, 2 Horror, 1 for a Pilgrim), Wendholt's only other piece of him, from Father Rook, if Pell was exposed.
  • A Reliquary key from Pell's belt, for the treasury of Saint Aubrel's. Keep it for a later adventure.
  • A Blank indulgence from Pell's coach. He had several.

On the Sacrament RPG Table

  • One scene per chapter, built with Prepare. Obstacles and countdowns as cards; enemies as tokens with the italic line as public text. Keep the Penitents hidden until they come over the side.
  • Give the bearer the catalogue relic Rib of the Hospice Saint. A Penitent laid to rest by it is healed by the table: remove its token by hand straight after.
  • Set the Target by hand as each scene goes live.
