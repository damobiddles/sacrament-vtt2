Privacy Policy

What the Sacrament RPG Table keeps, why, and how to ask for it to be removed.

Who we are

This website and the Sacrament RPG Table are run by Dungeon Jukebox, also known as Damien Bidmead. You can contact us at damien.bidmead@gmail.com.

The newsletter

When the newsletter is switched on, signing up needs your email address and a tick in the consent box. We use your address only to send you the newsletter, and do not sell or share it for any other purpose.

  • Why we can email you: because you asked to and ticked the box. You can change your mind at any time.
  • Leaving: every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also write to the contact address above. We will remove you.
  • Where it is kept: with the email service we choose. Nothing is collected until the signup is switched on.
  • How long: until you unsubscribe.

Playing at a table

The Sacrament RPG Table has no accounts. When you join a table you type a name, which is shown to the others at that table. A table keeps what the GM and players do in it (cards, notes, rolls, pictures the GM uploads) so that you can leave and come back. Your browser remembers which table you were at so it can put you back.

  • Who can see a table: people who have its code or link. Keep it private if you want it private.
  • How long tables are kept: a table that nobody has opened for 12 months may be deleted, with everything in it. A GM can also ask us to delete a table sooner.
  • Heroes: heroes you build on the My heroes page are kept in your own browser, not on our server, until you bring one to a table.
  • Pictures: pictures a GM uploads are stored with that table and can be seen by anyone who can see the table, and by anyone who has the picture's exact address.

Cookies, storage and tracking

The site sets no tracking cookies and runs no advertising or analytics. The table keeps a small note in your browser (which table you were at, and your place in it) so it can put you back, and the My heroes page keeps your heroes there too. You can clear these at any time through your browser settings.

Typefaces

The pages load their typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads. Google may see your internet address when this happens.

Donations

If you donate, you do it on Ko-fi's website, and Ko-fi and its payment providers handle your payment details under their own privacy policies. We never see your card details. Ko-fi tells us your name or display name, the amount and any message you leave.

Our hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify, which, like any web host, may keep ordinary technical logs (such as your address and the pages you asked for) for a short time.

Your rights

We are based in the United Kingdom, and UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) applies. You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and ask us to stop using it. Write to the contact address above.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

Children

The site is not aimed at young children.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, we will say so on the news page. The rules for using the site are in the Terms of Use.
