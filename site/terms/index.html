What the site is

The site describes Sacrament RPG, a tabletop roleplaying game, and offers the Sacrament RPG Table: a free tool for playing the game together in your browsers. It is an early version, built in the open. Features, rules and pages may change, pause or stop at any time, without notice.

Your tables and what you put in them

  • No accounts. Anyone with a table's code or invitation link can join that table. Keep the code private if you want the table private.
  • What you add is yours. Names, notes, cards, heroes and pictures you put into a table stay yours. You give us permission to store them and show them to the people at that table, only so the table works.
  • Only add what you have the right to share. Do not upload pictures or text you do not own or have permission to use.
  • The GM's job. The GM decides who is invited to a table and what goes on it.
  • Keep your own copies. We try to keep tables safe, but we cannot promise they will never be lost. A table that nobody has opened for 12 months may be deleted.
