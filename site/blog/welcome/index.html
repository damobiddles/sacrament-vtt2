Welcome to the Sacrament updates

This is where progress on Sacrament gets written down. Updates will be short, and each one says what changed and what to try.

Where things stand

  • The Basic Rules now have their own page, with short sections and a worked example of play.
  • The VTT is playable in the browser. Players join with a table code, see the map the GM is showing, move their own tokens, strike adversaries and keep a character sheet. Game masters build maps in private, share them when ready, and run adversaries, turns and the Looming Horror from one screen.
  • The Character Builder lets anyone build a character, save it in their browser and download it as a PDF.

Try it

Build a character, read the rules, then open the table and say hello.

Still to come

Playtests in small groups, more adversaries for the bestiary, printable character sheets and zines, and more of the setting. Everything here is a draft and will change as it is tested at the table.
