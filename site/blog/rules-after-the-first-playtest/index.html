The rules after the first playtest

We played a short adventure through a simulator, three characters against three adversaries and an NPC, and it found that the characters could not lose. In most fights the party finished the adversaries before one of them acted. The full run, with the numbers, is on the playtest page.

What changed

  • A turn without tokens. A turn is a free Move, one Action and one quick action, with one reaction a round. You can push for a second Action, and the Looming Horror rises by 1.
  • Adversaries act more. Every adversary now has a number of attacks per turn and hits harder, so armour is a reduction and not a wall. With one Action a turn the players do less damage, so adversaries have about 80% of their earlier HP.
  • Spells and weapons are priced alike. A damage spell says how many d6 it rolls, a weapon of level n deals nd6 and adds +n to the hit, and the Looming Horror is the price of magic.
  • The Looming Horror moves. It also rises when a Wound is filled and when a non-attack check fails.
  • Rests matter. Magic heals each character once between rests. A rest heals 1 Wound, and a rest in a safe place rolls a d6 to lower the Looming Horror.
  • Dying, critical hits and milestones are written down. At 12 harm you are dying: each round without healing you roll a d6, and a 1 or 2 means a Death Bargain, which can end in death or in a quest on your character sheet. A natural 20 always hits and rolls damage twice. Each milestone on the ladder has a d6 aftershock table.
  • Body, Mind and Spirit replace the four attributes. Each starts at +0. Passive abilities and gear change them (medium armour takes 1 from Mind, heavy takes 2). You roll a d20, add the score, and meet or beat the difficulty. A weapon adds its level to a Strike, and a damage spell adds its own level. A natural 20 always succeeds and is the critical hit; a natural 1 always fails.

All of it is in the Basic Rules, and the VTT and the character builder follow it. The character PDF now uses the site's own fonts.

Everything here is still a draft. If you find something that does not work, tell us.
