Notes from the Undervaults

Copied from the ledger of the Order of the Vault, Aubrecht. The brother who wrote it asks not to be named.

Third week of the Vigil. Two more taken in the Hollowmere fens: a Drowned Acolyte, quiet as a held breath, and a thing the trappers call a Stair Wraith. Neither will be fed this month. The Prior asks that the lamps on the lower stairs be kept lit. I have stopped asking why.
