Inventory

Everything a person can reach on playsacrament.com, plus what is left over in the project. Nothing has been deleted: anything archived is moved to the archive folder and its old address redirects. This page is for you to go through and tell me what to keep, merge or archive.

How to read it

  • Archive means moving something out of public reach, with its old address redirecting somewhere sensible. It does not mean deleting.
  • Merge means folding it into something else that does the same job.
  • Each item has a "Your decision" box, already set to my recommendation. Change the ones you disagree with, then press Copy my decisions at the bottom and paste the result to me.
  • Your choices stay in this browser only. Nothing is sent anywhere.

How this was checked (2026-10-20)

  • Every address was opened in a real browser against the live site, and every link on every page was followed. I also read all the project files.
  • "Works" means it loaded without errors, and (where the project has tests for it) the tests pass. It does not mean I used every feature.
  • I could not verify: the Sacrament table (it needs Google's servers, which my setup cannot reach), the designer sign-in (needs your passphrase), and the playtest signup (submitting would add a real entry).
  • I can see what links to a page from inside the site. I cannot see links in other people's messages, other websites or search results, and I cannot see what Google has indexed.
