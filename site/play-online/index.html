Sacrament RPG

Play online

The Sacrament RPG Table: one shared table in your browser for a GM and their players, with no accounts and nothing to install.

The Sacrament RPG Table rolls the dice, adds up checks against the Target, takes damage off the right foe and keeps the Horror, so the group can get on with the story. The GM makes the table and shares a code; the players join with it. The rules are the same as around a real table and in the solo game.
