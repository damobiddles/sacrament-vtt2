The Sacrament RPG Table rolls the dice, adds up checks against the Target, takes damage off the right foe and keeps the Horror, so the group can get on with the story. The GM makes the table and shares a code; the players join with it. The rules are the same as around a real table and in the solo game.

## Before your first game

[Start here](/how-to-play/) is a short checklist for players and GMs. When you want the detail, the [Player guide](/player-guide/) and [GM guide](/gm-guide/) explain every control and what the table does for you.

## Ready-made for your table

[Free Scenes](/scenes/) are prepared encounters with pictures, enemies and obstacles that you import into your own table. The [Current Loot Tables](/loot/) list every item, grimoire and relic the table knows.
