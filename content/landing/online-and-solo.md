The [Rules](/rules/) are the whole game. This page is only about the software: what the [Sacrament RPG Table](/play-online/) and the [solo game](/solo/) count, decide or limit for you. Around a real table you can skip it.

## At the online table

**Rolls and targets.** The server rolls every die. Select a foe or obstacle, then use a weapon or spell, and choose a target when asked. On a success, damage or Effort goes onto that target by itself. Looking at another card afterwards does not change the target. A roll keeps the rule it began with, even if a GM changes a setting while it waits.

**Turns.** Only the GM moves the turn on. Passing the last name in the order starts a new round, which ticks running timers and dying countdowns once. The table does not count every Move and Action: that stays between you and the GM.

**Push.** A player can Push only during their own turn, once. Without a turn order, or as an exception, the GM selects the hero and presses **Allow this Push (+1 Horror)** beside their latest failed roll.

**Paths.** Press **Fits my path** before a check to make it Easy, once a scene.

**Healing.** The table counts which healing spell or item has healed whom since the last rest, so you don't have to. A target at full health spends nothing. A failed check can use up a charge without counting as that hero's one healing from it. Undo healing and rests in reverse order when the table warns you.

**The Horror.** Thresholds announce themselves. The GM's **New adventure** button in the Table tab lets each one fire again. Horror has its own Undo.

**Limits the table keeps.** A single check bonus is capped at +10, and a two-handed weapon's +2 damage counts once. The table also counts the three Horror-tempting things a hero may carry, packed ones included.

**What the GM still decides.** Range, sight, cover, ammunition, reloads, Guard, Assist, Dazed, a weapon's listed manoeuvres, and anything a spell or relic does without a die. Item and spell notes say which steps the table takes for you.

**Special powers.** Rend the Veil asks you to choose its foes before the check; one damage roll lands on all of them, and one Undo takes it all back. A once-a-scene power is spent on the attempt, even a miss, and leaving the scene or reloading the page does not refill it. The GM's **Refresh scene abilities** starts a new encounter. The Executioner's Blade has its own **final blow**: Ultimate damage, 3 Horror, once a scene.

**House rules.** In **Table → House rules** the GM can change Effort dice, add automatic scene danger to monster attacks, or switch off the Ultimate die.

## In the solo game

The solo game is your GM. It uses the same rules and makes the GM's calls itself: each scene's Target, its foes and obstacles, when a place is dark, what Horror 7, 14 and 21 bring, and a rest after each scene.

- **Breaking away** runs on to the next scene. You leave behind what you found in this one, and the Horror rises 1 for running (no blood is paid for it). There is nowhere to run in a road's last scene.
- **Falling alone.** You are dying as at the table: 3 rounds; crawl away free, or pay 1 Horror to act anyway. A crawl that succeeds leaves the scene at 1 HP and leaves behind what you found in it; a failed crawl costs a round. Bandages cannot tend yourself.
- **Paths.** Each path names one score (a Knight's STR, a Scholar's INT). The first check on it in a scene is Easy.
- **Marks.** A road won is an adventure lived through: a hero from My heroes who comes home gets its mark ticked for them.
- **Graves.** A hero who falls is laid to rest at the end of the road: give their last words, and choose one thing they carried to leave. The next hero to take the road carries it from the start. Graves are kept in this browser, in the Chronicle.
- Spells and items that protect **an ally**, such as Ward, have no use alone.
