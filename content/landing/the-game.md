## What you play
Sacrament RPG is a tabletop roleplaying game. One person is the **Game Master (GM)**: they describe the world, play its people and monsters, and decide how the situation responds. Each other player plays a hero, saying what they do and making choices together.

The world is a dark age of stone churches, plague carts and printing presses. The Church orders everyday life. Beyond the consecrated boundaries lie the Unhallowed lands, unexplored ruins and roads that few choose to travel. Read [the setting](/setting/) for the world your hero knows.

## What happens at the table
The GM describes a problem. A player says what their hero tries. When the outcome is uncertain, roll a twenty-sided die and add the relevant score to meet the **Target**. If the amount matters, a smaller **Effort** die measures damage or progress. A simple success may need no further roll.

Ordinary weapons, including bows and crossbows, use **Weapons d6**. The rare, unreliable guns use **Guns d8**. Every two-handed weapon adds +2 damage. A **Heart** is ten hit points. Healing, equipment and the shared **Horror** give the group decisions beyond simply attacking again.

Healing spells have no casting Horror cost; other spells add 1. Any failed roll can be pushed on your turn for 1 Horror; a dying hero can pay 1 Horror to act anyway. The Horror strikes at 7, 14 and 21, once an adventure each. A successful natural 20 adds a plain Ultimate d12 to normal Effort when enabled. Crossbows and guns need a Move to reload. The [player guide](/player-guide/) and [GM guide](/gm-guide/) explain the current VTT rules in full.

## How the virtual table helps
The Sacrament RPG Table puts heroes, enemies, obstacles and scenes in one shared browser session. Players use their character sheets; the GM prepares scenes and keeps private notes. Rolls and supported item effects are shared and saved by the table.

The GM still decides range, esoteric effects and what the Horror does. Read the guides for the boundary between automatic mechanics and table rulings.

## What you need
- One GM and a group of players, with a browser and an internet connection for the VTT.
- A table invitation from the GM, or a new table made from **Run a game**.
- A hero created on [My heroes](/character/), or a hero the GM provides.
- A way to talk to each other, in person or through your usual voice call. The VTT does not provide voice chat.

You can also play without a screen: [Play in person](/play-in-person/) has printable sheets, a ready-to-run adventure and a list of what to bring.

No account or installation is needed. Sacrament is being playtested, so the rules and tools will continue to develop. Follow [News](/news/) and send [feedback](/feedback/) after your session.
