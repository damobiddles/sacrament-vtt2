---
title: Help improve the table
summary: Report a bug, share an idea or tell us what you like. No account or email address needed.
---
A few sentences are enough. For a bug, describe what you tried and what happened. For an idea, tell us what would make your next session better.

We review submissions privately to plan fixes and improvements. The form does not ask for contact details, so it does not provide a personal reply. Please leave out private GM links, access keys and other people’s personal information.
