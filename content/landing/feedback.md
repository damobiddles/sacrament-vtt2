---
title: Help improve the table
summary: Found a bug? Tell us what happened, what you enjoy and what you would change.
---
You do not need an account to send feedback. A few sentences are enough, whether you have played a whole session or only just opened the table.

## Something went wrong
Tell us what you were trying to do, what you expected and what happened instead. If you can, include the steps to repeat it and your device and browser.

## What do you like or dislike?
Tell us what felt easy, what got in the way and what would make your next session better. Small annoyances and good moments are both useful.

Only fill in the parts of the message that apply. Please keep private GM links, access keys and other players' personal information out of messages and screenshots.
