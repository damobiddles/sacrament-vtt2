Sacrament is a conversation: the GM describes a situation, the players say what their heroes do, and dice settle uncertain outcomes. You can learn as you play. No account or installation is needed.

## I am a player

1. **Get an invitation.** Ask the GM for their invitation link or six-letter table code.
2. **Create a hero.** Open [My heroes](/character/) in this browser, choose a starting focus and press **Generate a hero**. Change the name and vow if you like; press **Keep this hero** to save it. The generator supplies scores, Effort and a starting kit.
3. **Join the table.** Open the invitation or [enter the code](/vtt/?join), then enter your player name. Choose **Bring a hero** to copy your saved hero into this game, or **Play** beside a hero your GM has provided.
4. **Find your actions.** Open **My character**, then **Ready to use**. Select an enemy before using a weapon. A successful attack leads to **Roll damage**; the result applies to the chosen target automatically. Tell the group what you are doing and let the GM guide your first roll.

[Read the player guide: scores, equipment, attacks, healing and Horror](/player-guide/)

## I am the GM

1. **Make a table.** Open [Run a table](/vtt/?run). Start with the sample encounter to learn the controls, or untick it to begin empty.
2. **Keep your access safe.** In the **Table** tab, save the private GM recovery link. Keep it to yourself. Use the separate **Copy invite link** to invite players.
3. **Prepare a simple encounter.** In **Scene**, add an enemy and an obstacle. Keep unrevealed cards hidden. In **Play**, arrange the turn order. Set the Target and explain any house rules.
4. **Walk through a first turn.** Have a player select the enemy, use a weapon, roll damage and see the result applied automatically. The GM then presses **Next turn**. The footer's **GM dice** can roll shared or private dice without changing HP.

## The five rules to remember first

- **Checks:** d20 + a score must meet the Target. Easy lowers the Target by 3; Hard raises it by 3.
- **Effort:** a second die measures damage or progress when needed. Ordinary weapons, bows and crossbows use d6; guns use d8.
- **Healing:** use it on yourself or a valid target. Supported catalogue healing updates HP, charges and rest limits automatically. The GM checks range.
- **Horror:** healing spells have no casting Horror cost; other spells add 1. Items state their own cost. A hero's natural 1 and a Push each add 1 separately.
- **Push:** offered only after a failed attack, once per turn, for an immediate second action. That action cannot be pushed again.

## Returning, backups and help

Return to the same table address in the same browser to resume. **Your tables** on the VTT entry page lists games remembered on that device. GMs can use **Back to my tables** without forgetting their access; **Forget** is a separate action. On another browser, players rejoin and ask the GM to reassign their existing hero; GMs use their private recovery link.

My heroes saves locally, not to an account. Download a private heroes backup before clearing browser data or changing device. On My heroes, select that JSON file under **Restore a heroes backup**, review the preview and press **Restore backup**. Your existing heroes are kept. Bringing a hero into a game creates a separate copy; later changes to the local hero do not update it.

The table handles shared rolls and supported mechanics. The GM decides range, esoteric effects, free-text custom effects and Horror consequences. Both guides distinguish those rulings from automation. The rules are still being playtested.
