---
title: How to play here
summary: A plain-English guide to the Sacrament RPG Table, for players and for GMs.
---
This is a guide to what the table does today. It is an early version, and the rules it follows are still being worked out, so a few details may change.

## If you are a player

### Joining
Your GM will send you either an **invitation link** or a **six-letter table code**. Open the link and type your name, or go to [the join page](/vtt/?join), type the code and your name, and press **Join**. There is nothing to download and no account to make.

### Getting a hero
After you join, the panel on the side shows the heroes nobody has taken yet. Press **Play** next to the one you want. If none are free, ask your GM to add one for you.

### Your sheet
Press **Sheet** on your strip (or **Open your sheet** when you select your own token) to open your hero full-screen over the table. The table keeps running underneath, and closing the sheet puts you straight back. On it you can change your scores (6 points to share, no more than 6 in one), your Effort (4 points), your gear (ten slots equipped, ten in the pack) and write your appearance, background and notes. If a rule says no, the sheet tells you why in plain words. You can choose one grimoire yourself; relics and further books are finds your GM gives you. Spells and magic items are marked **Tempts the Horror**. Supplies (rope, bandages, candles, loot such as a signet ring) work from the pack: they show a **Supply** label instead of an Equip button. Only you and the GM can read your background and notes. The GM can open and change any hero's sheet.

You can also build heroes between sessions on the [My heroes](/character/) page, which saves them in your browser. At a table with no hero yet, press **Bring a hero** to copy one from there: the table checks it against the rules and makes it yours.

### Using an item
When you click an enemy or obstacle, your strip asks **What are you using?** and lists your weapons, spells and tools. Choose one (or press **Use** beside an item on your sheet). The table makes the check with the item's stat. If you succeed, press **Roll damage** (**Roll Effort** against an obstacle): it rolls that item's own kind of **Effort**, adding your bonus in that kind. Then press **Apply** as usual. If you click a stat while aiming at something, the strip checks that you meant to use no item first. A weapon, relic or grimoire must be equipped first (a grimoire is used through one of its spells). A flask is used up when you use it. Items with no Effort kind, such as a cloak or a rope, never roll.

### Your strip
The bar along the bottom is yours. It shows your hero's hearts, with **−** and **+** to change your own hit points, and a button for each of your six stats. Under them you can choose **easy**, **normal** or **hard** for your next roll, open your **Sheet** or **Items**, or press **More** for **Effort** and **My dice**.

### Rolling
1. Click the thing you are trying to hit or beat, if there is one (an enemy or an obstacle).
2. Click a stat. The table rolls a twenty-sided die for you and shows the sum in a sentence, for example "12 + 3 STR = 15 against Target 12. Success."
3. If you succeeded, roll the damage: choose a kind of **Effort** (Basic, Weapons, Guns, Magic or Ultimate). It rolls and shows the total.
4. Press **Apply to** the card shown. The enemy's hearts go down, or the obstacle's Effort bar fills. Nothing changes until you press Apply.

The card the Effort will go to is marked **TARGET**. Looking at another card does not move it. To change it on purpose, select the other card and press **Change target**.

### Using your own dice
Press **More**, then **My dice**, and type what your physical d20 shows before you click a stat, or type the Effort die in the box under the Effort buttons. The table marks those results as "physical die".

### Ending your turn, and taking things back
Press **End turn** when you are done. If you pressed something by mistake, **Undo last** (bottom right) takes back your last action. You can only undo your own.

### Staying connected
If your connection drops the page says so and keeps trying. If you close the page by accident, open the table's address again and you are back at the table as the same hero. **Leave**, at the top right, takes you out of the table on this device; you can join again with the code.

## If you are the GM

### Making a table
Go to [the run page](/vtt/?run) and press **Make a table**. Leave "Start with the sample encounter" ticked for a ready-made example, or untick it to start empty. You get a six-letter code. Open the **Table** tab in the tray and press **Copy invite link**, then send the link to your players.

### Heroes
Open the **Table** tab in the tray. **Players** lists who has joined and lets you choose their hero. **Add a blank hero** makes a new one: give it a name and fill in its numbers on its **Edit card** screen. You can also roll for any hero yourself: select the hero's token, or press **Roll as …** at the bottom.

### Cards and tokens
Open the **Scene** tab and press **Enemy**, **Obstacle** or **Card**. Click a card on the table to see it in the tray: its hit points, Effort bar or countdown, and whether players can see it. **Edit card** holds its name, description, private notes only you can see, a picture (a web address, or an upload), and more. Drag a card to move it. Tokens, hearts and bars update for everyone.

### What players can and cannot see
A card that is **Hidden from players** does not exist for them: nothing about it is even sent to their devices. **Hide the numbers from players** (on **Edit card**) shows the card but not its hit points or counters, only that it has some. Press **Show to players** when you want to show something.

### The tray
With nothing selected, the tray has four tabs: **Play** (the turn order), **Scene** (scenes and what you can add to the table), **Loot** and **Table** (the invite link, players, house rules). Click a card and the tray shows that card instead, with a **Back** link. Players never see any of this.

### Scenes
Open the **Scene** tab. Type a name and press **Add**, then **Open** the new scene: you are now working in a scene your players cannot see, with a banner saying so. Add cards, move things, set a picture (**Change the picture for this scene**). When it is ready, press **Show to players** and everyone moves to it. You can go back to an earlier scene the same way, and everything in it is as you left it.

### Turns, rounds and countdowns
The **Turn order** on the **Play** tab decides who goes when; tap a name to make it their turn, or press **Edit order** to move, remove or add places. **End turn** moves on, and when the last place finishes a new round starts. A countdown card (add a **Timer** to any card) loses one each round; when it reaches zero everyone sees it and you get an alert. Press **Resolved** once you have dealt with it.

### Monster attacks
Click a monster, then press **Attack a hero…** in the tray. Set its **Attack bonus** (blank counts as +0; if the scene is dangerous, put that in the bonus yourself), choose the hero, and press **Attack**. The table rolls a twenty-sided die, adds the bonus, and compares it with the hero's **Defence**: 10 + their CON + their armour (armour counts for at most +5). Each hero's Defence is shown on their strip and in the hero's panel. On a miss, nothing happens. On a hit, press a kind of **Effort**: it is rolled and taken off the hero's hit points at once, and you can **Undo** it in the Activity feed. Everyone sees the attack, the roll and the damage in the feed. A hero who reaches 0 is marked **AT 0** for everyone. A monster you have hidden is shown to players as "Something", with no name. Only you can make monster attacks.

### The loot table
Open the **Loot** tab and press **Roll loot (d100)**, or type what your own d100 shows. Most numbers lead to a smaller table with its own die (supplies, arms and tools, valuables and curios, or relics), which is rolled for you; with your own dice, type both numbers with a space, such as 87 4. Only you see the roll. The find appears with its description and, for a few, a note meant for you alone. Press **Put it on the table (hidden)** to make a card from it, or choose a hero and **Give it to this hero** to put it straight on their sheet. Players cannot pick these items for themselves.

### Loot
Press **Show to players** on a hidden card to put it on the table, or open **Edit card** and **Give to player** to hand it to one person. It then appears in their gear and leaves the table.

### The Rising Horror
The **Horror** meter in the top bar runs from 0 to 21 and is the same for everyone. Casting a spell, or using a relic or other item that tempts the Horror, raises it by that thing's amount, whether the roll succeeds or not. Some amounts are rolled when used (such as d4+2), and the activity feed shows the roll. A hero's d20 that shows a **natural 1** raises it by 1 by itself. After a **failed roll** you are offered a **Push**: press it and the Horror rises by 1, and you take a second action straight away (the same roll again, or anything else). You can push once a turn, and a pushed action cannot be pushed again. At **7**, **14** and **21** the feed announces it and the GM is alerted. At 21 the Horror has arrived, and everyone is told. Only the GM can lower the meter, reset it or take back a rise: in the **Table** tab, or with **Undo** in the activity feed.

The **Grimoire with no title** has one button, **Read**: the table rolls a d4 and you cast whichever of its four spells comes up.

### Pointing and drawing
**Point** (top left of the table, then press and hold) shows everyone where you mean. **Draw** lets you sketch on the table; **Clear drawing** wipes it.

## On a phone
The table works on a phone: the tray sits under the table and scrolls by itself.

## What it does not do yet
- **No distances or ranges.** Range is whatever the map picture shows. The table does not measure it.
- **The Horror is counted, not played.** The table raises the Horror meter and tells the GM at each threshold, but the GM decides what the Horror does.
- **Healing is by hand.** The table keeps the dying countdown (a d4 at 0, a round off each new round) and the GM's **Party rest**, but healing from bandages and prayers, and its once-per-rest limit, you run yourselves: change hit points with the **−** and **+** controls, and tell the story.
- **No accounts.** A table is its code. If a player loses their device they rejoin as a new person and the GM can give them back their hero.
- **The rules are not final.** They are still being worked out, so a few details may change.
