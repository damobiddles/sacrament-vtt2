---
title: Current Loot Tables
summary: The current Sacrament RPG loot tables, equipment and three-spell grimoires.
---
Roll d100 on the main table, then roll d100 again on the table it points to. Each number has one result. The catalogue below also includes starting equipment and items the GM can award directly.

Spells are labelled **Damage**, **Healing** or **Esoteric effect**. Damage spells roll Effort; other effects do what their description says. The GM still resolves effects the table does not automate, including healing adjustments. A grimoire holds three spells; some spells appear in more than one book. The sixteen books draw from twenty-three different spells.

These are the shared catalogue items, not the private loot or notes from anyone's game. This page updates from the same catalogue as the VTT whenever the site is published.
