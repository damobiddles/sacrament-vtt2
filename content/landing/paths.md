A **path** is the kind of life your hero has led: Knight or Cutpurse, Scholar or Witch. It is optional. Choose one on the character sheet, under **Who**, or leave it empty. You can change it later.

[Rules](/rules/) · [Player guide](/player-guide/) · [Loot tables](/loot/#table-pathrelics) · [My heroes](/character/)

## What a path does

A path does **one thing**. Each path has its own **path relics**: magic things that people of that path have made, carried or stolen. Any hero can carry and use any path relic, but they are hungry things. Each use costs **2 or 3 Horror**, whether it works or not, and **1 less for a hero of its own path**. A path gives no scores, no Effort and no gear. Your calling and vow still say who your hero is in your own words; the path is the part the rules know about.

## Path relics and the Horror

Every path relic is forbidden magic, even in the hands of a Diplomat or a Knight. Using one tempts the Horror like any relic. A natural 1 adds Horror only when the relic is used to attack, and past 21 each point of Horror that cannot go on the meter costs the hero d4 HP. Many path relics have only a few uses, counted on the sheet: when the last is spent they break, dry up or burn out.

The Church holds that magic tempts the Horror, and it does not forgive those who use it. The Inquisition keeps lists. A hero who draws on relics too often, or brings the Horror down on a village, may find a summons waiting. That is the GM's story to tell, not a rule.

## Finding them

There are **100 path relics**, five for each path. On the loot tables, a d100 of 81 to 95 leads to the Relics table, and 67 to 100 there lead to the Path relics table, where each number is one relic. The GM can also give a fitting path relic as a reward. See them all on the [loot page](/loot/#loot-path).
