We have just run **The Rib of Saint Hussey** around a table, and it is the kind of evening we want Sacrament to be good at: short, fast, and a little bit grim by the end. Now it is packed up so you can run it too, with paper, pencils and dice, no screens needed.

## The adventure

A hundred years ago, when the sickness came to the city of Aubrecht, a lay brother called Hussey walked in from the village of Wendholt with a handcart and carried the dead out for forty days. Then he caught it, walked home and died. The city made him a saint, dug him up, and kept his bones in its cathedral, where the sick pay good coin to be let near them.

Wendholt has finally won one of them back. The heroes are hired to carry the saint's rib to his chapel and lay it in his tomb by dusk on his feast day. It is a hard day's road east, over the River Wend. Somebody in the cathedral would very much prefer that it never arrives.

It is five scenes, for three to five starting heroes, in about two hours:

1. **The Snatch.** A chase through the city, over fish carts and across the roofs.
2. **The Gate.** The watch are looking for relic thieves, and the description sounds a lot like you.
3. **The Riders.** A polite man with a crossbow, a generous offer, and a hay wagon with nobody steering it.
4. **Brackford Ferry.** A flat barge on a rope, a high brown river, and the light going.
5. **The Feast at Wendholt.** Bonfires, fiddles, a pig on the spit, and four hundred people waiting for their saint.

It uses the current rules: a Target for each scene, Effort against obstacles, countdowns, catalogue loot and the Horror meter. The rib is the catalogue relic Rib of the Hospice Saint, exactly as written, and a hero who walks the Barber-surgeon's path pays less Horror to use it. A few rulings belong to this adventure only, such as how the drowned dead can be laid to rest, and the text marks them as GM choices, not rules.

## The GM pack

**Everything in the pack is for the GM.** It tells you who is behind it all and what waits at the river. If you are going to play it, stop reading here and let your GM download it.

[Download the GM pack](/downloads/adventures/the-rib-of-saint-hussey-gm-pack.zip) (zip, 2.4 MB). It has a folder for A4 and a folder for US Letter, each with:

- **The adventure**, the whole thing as a printable PDF: the job, the people, read-aloud text, every foe, obstacle and countdown, the chase table, the endings and the rewards.
- **The plot on one page**, to glance at during play.
- **GM cards**, one page per scene, with every obstacle, countdown and foe as a card to cut out, and boxes to cross off for damage, Effort and rounds.
- **A blank hero sheet**, one for each player.
- **The rules cheat sheet**, with checks, Effort, Push, the Horror, healing, dying and paths.

The adventure is also here on its own as a PDF ([A4](/downloads/adventures/the-rib-of-saint-hussey-A4.pdf) or [US Letter](/downloads/adventures/the-rib-of-saint-hussey-Letter.pdf)), and you can [read it online](/adventures/the-rib-of-saint-hussey/), where you can also roll on the chase table.

## For the players

The players do not need anything from the pack except a hero and the cheat sheet. They can [make a hero on My heroes](/character/) and print it, or fill in the blank sheet by hand. There are no player handouts: the GM reads the opening aloud.

## Running it

Bring a set of dice for each player, pencils with erasers, and a coin to mark the Horror. The [Play in person](/play-in-person/) page has the full list, who keeps track of what at a real table, and the rules on one page. Our first in-person adventure, [The Guest at Greave](/adventures/the-guest-at-greave/), is there too, if your table wants a longer and colder evening afterwards.
