## Play solo: The Pilgrim Road

You no longer need a GM, or even a group, to play Sacrament. **[Play solo](/solo/)** is a short game for one hero, in your browser. A road is short enough to finish in one sitting.

You walk the pilgrim road towards the boundary of the consecrated land. Every road is five scenes: an opening that gives you a reason to go (a relic to carry, a missing sister to find, or an Inquisitor's warrant), three scenes picked from eleven along the way, and a last scene that answers why you came. The road forks, night falls as you go, and the last scene is always in the dark.

![The solo game: a hooded pilgrim before an Inquisitor's desk, with HP, the Target and the Horror under the picture, buttons for each choice, and the story on the right.](/art/news/the-pilgrim-road-solo.webp)

*The Inquisitor's Warrant, the first scene of one road. A friend on the road shows in green.*

- **The real rules.** Solo uses the same rules, items and spells as the online table and play on paper, and a test checks they agree. The game plays the GM: it sets the Target, says when it is dark and decides what a failure costs. Everything else is the rules you would use at a table, so a road is a good way to learn them.
- **Your hero, or a new one.** Bring a hero from [My heroes](/character/), or take a ready-made one: choose a path, or Surprise me.
- **Friends on the road.** Up to two strangers turn up on each road, their names in green, and each helps once. One of them is a necromancer with some very impressive invisible bats.
- **Pictures and music.** Every place is drawn like a page from an illuminated book, and each has its own small tune. The music is off until you turn it on.
- **Something to show for it.** Win a road and you can bring your finds home to My heroes, and your hero earns a mark (more on marks below). A chronicle keeps each hero's roads and deeds, and after three wins the Hard Road opens: a higher Target, tougher foes and one more scene.

## Twenty paths

A hero may now follow a **path**: the life they led before the road. There are twenty, for the forbidden crafts and the ordinary trades of the age alike:

Druid, Necromancer, Hunter, Assassin, Mercenary, Priest, Witch, Penitent, Barber-surgeon, Witch-finder, Scholar, Diplomat, Knight, Merchant, Pilgrim, Cutpurse, Minstrel, Gravedigger, Alchemist and Sailor.

A path does one thing, and it is a big one: **a relic of your own path costs 1 less Horror** each time you use it. It gives no scores, Effort or gear, and you can leave it empty. Choose it on the character sheet, at the table, on My heroes or in the solo game. The new [Paths](/rules/paths/) page explains them and lists every relic that belongs to each.

## The loot bag

- **A hundred new path relics,** five for each path, on the new Path relics table (67 to 100 on the Relics table). The chance of finding a relic at all has not changed.
- **Every relic has a path now,** 128 in all. The older relics cost 1 more Horror, so a hero of the right path pays what everyone paid before. The Penitent's Chain costs 2, or 1 for a Penitent.
- **Relics that do what they say.** The Penitent's Chain applies itself at the table: d4 to you, +3 on your next roll, once a turn, and Undo takes it back. In the solo game the Chain, the Bone Bell and the Censer of Ash all work on their own.
- **Plain finds work like their twins.** Tallow candle stubs light like candles, hemp rope climbs like rope and grapnel, and the gravedigger's spade is now a proper weapon. Seven relics also now say which check they use.
- **Grimoires.** There are now 22 books and 66 spells. Every book still holds exactly three spells, and no spell appears in two books. Heroes who own an older edition keep it as it was.
- **Story spells have answers.** Spells and items with no damage die now carry tags (Light, Hide, Bind, Silence and eight more), each with a default effect in a fight and out of one. When a spell's words leave it open, the tag is the quick answer.

Everything is on the [Current Loot Tables](/loot/), grouped by path.

## Heroes grow stronger

**Advancement is here.** Each adventure a hero lives through to its end earns 1 mark. At 3 marks the hero takes an advance: +1 to one score (up to 6) or +1 to one Effort bonus (up to +10), and the marks start again. The character sheet has a new Marks box, the printed hero sheet has one too, and in the solo game a road won ticks the mark for you.

## Rules that changed

Sacrament is still being playtested, so the rules move. Each change is recorded, with the reason, on [Rules updates and errata](/rules/updates/). The biggest:

- **The Horror is sharper.** A natural 1 adds Horror only on an attack, a spell or a pushed action, not on a plain check. And once the meter is full at 21, **you pay in blood:** each point that cannot go on the meter costs the hero who caused it d4 HP.
- **Break away.** Instead of acting, a hero can try to get out of a fight with a DEX check against the Target. Succeed, and the foe does not get a parting blow.
- **Darkness.** Without a light, a WIS check to search or notice in a dark place is Hard. Nothing else.
- **Playing alone.** The Rules page has a short section on what is different when you play solo.

## A tidier website

- **A clearer menu.** Eight items: The game, The setting, Start here, Rules, Play online, Play solo, Play in person and Resources. News and Feedback live at the bottom of every page.
- **Play online** is the one place to join a game, run one, open My heroes or go back to your tables.
- **The rules pages share one row of tabs:** Rules, Paths, Player guide, GM guide and Updates and errata.
- **A top-to-bottom check** of the site, the table, the solo game and the loot, so they all say the same thing. The fixes are on the updates page.
