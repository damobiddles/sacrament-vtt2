## What Sacrament is

Sacrament is a dark fantasy tabletop role-playing game. The Binding holds the demons beneath the earth, the Church keeps the Binding, and magic is forbidden. Your heroes use it anyway, and every spell brings the Horror a little closer.

The rules are light. You roll a d20, add a stat and try to reach the Target. When you hurt something you roll Effort. There is a short guide for players and GMs in [Start here](/how-to-play/), and the full rules are on the [Rules](/rules/) page.

## The table

The Sacrament RPG Table is one shared table in your browser. The GM makes a table and shares a code; the players join with it. There are no accounts and nothing to install.

- **It does the arithmetic.** The table rolls the dice, adds up each check against the Target, takes damage off the right foe and keeps the Horror.
- **The GM keeps their secrets.** Notes, hidden foes and private rolls stay on the GM's screen.
- **Same rules everywhere.** The table, the solo game and play on paper use the same rules, items and spells.
